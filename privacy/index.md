# Privacy Notice

Last updated: 22 August 2026

This is the privacy notice for brewerlogic.com, the personal portfolio of Adam Brewer. The site exists to describe my professional experience, publish engineering case studies and let people contact me about relevant work. I am responsible for the personal data described below. Questions or requests can be sent to adam@brewerlogic.com.

## Information collected

The site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate activity such as pages visited, approximate location, device type and referral source. Google may set or read cookies and process technical identifiers under its own privacy terms. The hosting and delivery providers may also retain routine request logs containing an IP address, browser details, requested URL and timestamp for security, reliability and abuse prevention.

If you email me, I receive the contact details and information you choose to include. There is no account system, contact form, advertising profile or sale of personal information on this site. Please do not send sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your enquiry.

## Why information is used

Analytics information is used to understand whether the portfolio is useful and to identify broad traffic trends. Technical logs are used to operate, secure and troubleshoot the site. Enquiry information is used to reply, discuss potential work and retain an appropriate business record. The relevant basis is consent where the law requires it, and otherwise my legitimate interests in operating a secure professional website and responding to enquiries.

## Sharing and retention

Information may be processed by service providers that support the site, including Google Analytics and DigitalOcean App Platform. Those providers may process data outside the UK under their own safeguards. I do not sell personal information. Analytics and technical records follow the retention controls of the relevant provider. Direct correspondence is kept only while it remains useful for the enquiry, a professional relationship, or legal and accounting obligations.

## Your choices and rights

You can block or delete cookies through your browser and use browser privacy controls or content blockers. Depending on where you live, you may ask for access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or an export of personal information, and may object to certain processing. Email adam@brewerlogic.com to make a request. UK users can also raise unresolved concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office at https://ico.org.uk/.

## External links and changes

This site links to third-party services and projects. Their privacy practices apply after you follow those links. I may update this notice when the site, its providers or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the current version.
